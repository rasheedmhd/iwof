The Inshallah Widows and Orphans Foundation is proud to announce a special Charity Concert & CEO Homecoming celebration, set to take place on 2nd January 2027 at Zebango Lodge, Zebilla.

The Chief Executive Officer of the Foundation, Mr. Awudu Fuseini, popularly known as Top Senior, is set to celebrate his homecoming following a remarkable year of commitment to supporting widows, orphans, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups across the Kusaug Traditional Area.

The homecoming celebration will provide an opportunity to reflect on the Foundation's journey, recognise its achievements, and appreciate the individuals and partners who have supported its humanitarian mission throughout 2026.

Since its establishment, the Foundation has been committed to restoring hope, improving livelihoods, and promoting the welfare of vulnerable people through donations, community support, and empowerment initiatives.

The event will celebrate Mr. Awudu Fuseini Top Senior's vision of building a society where vulnerable individuals are valued, supported, and empowered to live with dignity. It will also serve as a platform to rally community members, traditional leaders, stakeholders and development partners around the Foundation's ongoing mission.

As the Foundation celebrates this milestone, it calls on all community members to join hands in advancing its mission of compassion, unity and sustainable support for those in need.

For more information, call: 0548 075 413

Registration • August 2026

Foundation Registers 6,132 Beneficiaries Across Kusaug

Inshallah Widows and Orphans Foundation — Official Update

The Inshallah Widows and Orphans Foundation has successfully completed its screening and registration exercise across the six districts of Kusaug, reaching thousands of vulnerable individuals, including widows, orphans, and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs).

The exercise was aimed at identifying deserving beneficiaries, creating a reliable database, and ensuring that future support programmes are targeted at those who need assistance most. Through the commitment of foundation officials, volunteers, community leaders and other stakeholders, a total of 6,132 beneficiaries have been registered across the six districts.

"Every widow, orphan and Person with Disability deserves dignity, inclusion and an opportunity to improve their quality of life."

Foundation Statement • 27 July 2026

Our Commitment Remains: Service to Humanity Cannot Wait

Inshallah Widows and Orphans Foundation — Official Statement

The Inshallah Widows and Orphans Foundation was established with one clear mission: to improve the lives of widows and orphans through compassion, empowerment, and practical support. Since its inception, the Foundation has demonstrated a commitment to serving vulnerable members of society, regardless of political affiliation.

On 16 July 2026 in Binduri and 26 July 2026 in Garu, the Foundation successfully held its launch events. We invited our District Chief Executives (DCEs), Members of Parliament (MPs), and other public officials because we wanted them to witness this important initiative and share in the vision of transforming lives.

Unfortunately, many of our invited leaders were unable to attend. While we respect that public officials have demanding schedules, their presence at such events would have sent a powerful message that the welfare of widows and orphans is a priority for all of us.

"Our invitation was never about politics or campaigning. We simply wanted our leaders to witness what ordinary citizens are doing to support vulnerable people."

Community development is a shared responsibility, and when government, civil society, traditional authorities, and volunteers work together, everyone benefits.

Despite the challenges, our CEO, Awudu Fuseini (Top Senior), the management team, and our volunteers remain committed to this mission. We will continue to reach out to those in need because service to humanity cannot wait.

Widows and orphans should never become an afterthought or a political issue. They deserve our compassion, our time, and our collective support.

The future of Kusaug is in our hands. If we unite behind initiatives that improve lives, we can build a stronger, more caring society where every widow feels supported and every orphan has hope for a brighter tomorrow.
