Who We Are

Caring for Kusaug's most vulnerable — with compassion and purpose

At Inshallah Widows and Orphans Foundation, we restore hope, dignity and happiness to widows, orphans and Persons with Disabilities through love, care and community support. We have already registered 6,132 beneficiaries across all six districts of Kusaug.

What We Do

Support that meets real, everyday needs

We provide essential support and create opportunities for widows and orphans to recover, grow and thrive — from immediate relief to long-term empowerment.

Food & Basic Needs

Regular food support and essential household items to ease daily hardship for families in need.

Shelter & Clothing

Helping widows and orphans secure safe housing and access to clothing for themselves and their children.

Healthcare Access

Connecting families to medical care and health support so health challenges don't go unaddressed.

Education Support

Helping orphans stay in school with support for fees, supplies, and a path toward a better future.

Skills Training & Empowerment

Vocational training and economic empowerment programs that help widows become self-reliant.

Counseling & Mentorship

Emotional guidance and mentorship to help widows and orphans overcome trauma, loneliness and hardship.
